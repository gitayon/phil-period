# Philosophy Department, 19th Batch

> “I study what is, what ought to be, and why my timetable has become a metaphysical crisis.”

I am a philosophy student with a strong interest in logic, ethics, critical thought, and the beautiful chaos of trying to understand reality while also remembering which room the seminar is in.

## About Me

- Major: Philosophy
- Batch: 19
- Motto: “Reason first, panic second.”
- Specialty: turning reading lists into emotional support systems
- Favorite activity: asking deeper questions while pretending I am “just checking the syllabus”

## My Philosophy Stack

- Logic: because a sound argument is a kind of moral hygiene
- Ethics: because “should” is a much more dangerous word than “is”
- Metaphysics: what is real, what is not, and why my Wi‑Fi keeps disappearing
- Epistemology: how we know things, and why I still forget the date of the exam
- Political philosophy: power, justice, and the eternal suffering of group projects

## A Typical Day

```text
08:00 — Wake up, stare at the ceiling, ask existential questions
09:00 — Class begins; I already have three thoughts and zero notes
11:00 — Debate with myself over whether the text is clear or just unreadable
13:00 — Lunch + dramatic philosophical reflection on hunger and desire
15:00 — Readings, highlights, and mild suffering
18:00 — Return to dorm, convert confusion into notes
21:00 — “I understand the concept now” (a false statement spoken optimistically)
23:00 — Sleep, dream of being accepted into the world of clear arguments
```

## What I Actually Do

- Read difficult texts and pretend I am calmly processing them
- Search for the meaning of life in lecture slides
- Build arguments like they are architectural plans for my future
- Keep a running list of questions I still cannot answer
- Laugh at paradoxes, then immediately become one

## My Core Beliefs

- Every idea deserves a fair hearing, even if it is deeply unhinged
- A good question is often more valuable than a rushed answer
- The soul should be disciplined, but the mind must still be curious
- A philosopher is not someone who knows everything; it is someone who knows how to ask better questions

## The Real Curriculum

Beyond the classroom, I am learning:

- how to think clearly under pressure
- how to argue without losing my dignity
- how to survive a reading list that looks like a small mountain
- how to find meaning in routine, structure, and ordinary life

## Favorite Philosophical Mood

- “I am not lazy; I am in a deep state of contemplative inquiry.”
- “This is not procrastination. This is incubation.”
- “I am not behind; I am building a relationship with the material.”

## Current Vibe

I am not just studying philosophy. I am living with it.

I want my life to be less chaotic, more intentional, and at least a little more rational than my browser tabs.

## Closing Thought

If life is a question, then I am trying to become a better student of it.

And if the question is too hard, I will still show up, read the text, take notes, and keep thinking.
